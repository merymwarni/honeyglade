What Is English Mystery Academy?

English Mystery Academy is an interactive English learning website designed for junior high school students. It combines learning materials, educational games, and mystery-based challenges to create an enjoyable learning experience.

🎯

Our Purpose

This website aims to help students develop their English skills, including listening, speaking, reading, and writing through interactive and engaging activities.

✨

Our Concept

Inspired by a detective academy, students explore learning missions, complete educational games, and solve challenges to become successful English detectives.

Listening Mission

Critical Listening Challenge

Listen carefully, analyze the speaker's experience, identify important information, and develop your own conclusions about solo travelling as a student.

Mission 1: Listen to the Story

Listen to the audio entitled “The Mystery at the Forest Campsite”. You may listen more than once, but try to understand the speaker's ideas before answering the questions.

💡

Detective Tip: Do not focus only on individual words. Pay attention to the speaker's main ideas, reasons, experiences, problems, and conclusions.

Mission 2

Critical Thinking Questions

Complete all 8 questions based on the audio. Identify important information, analyze the mystery, draw conclusions, and develop your own ideas.

Understanding the Main Idea

1. What is the main focus of the audio?

Identifying Specific Information

2. Identify two important clues or details from the audio that help the characters understand the mystery. Explain why each detail is important.

Analyzing the Sequence of Events

3. Describe how the mystery develops throughout the story. What happened at the beginning, what happened next, and how did the story end?

Making an Inference

4. What can you infer about the characters' feelings or personalities from their reactions to the mystery? Give at least two reasons based on the audio.

Using Evidence

5. Which event or detail provides the strongest evidence for understanding what happened at the campsite? Explain how this evidence helps solve the mystery.

Evaluating Decisions

6. Do you think the characters handled the mysterious situation appropriately? Evaluate their decisions and explain what they could have done better.

Problem Solving

7. Imagine you were at the same forest campsite and experienced a similar mysterious situation. What would you do to stay safe and investigate the situation responsibly? Explain at least two actions.

Creating and Giving Recommendations

8. Imagine your friend wants to go camping in a forest for the first time. Based on the ideas from the audio and your own knowledge, what advice would you give? Provide at least three practical recommendations and explain why they are important.

Speaking Mission

Speaking Challenge

Watch the mystery video, explore the clues, and answer ten challenging questions orally to improve your English communication skills.

Mission 1: Watch and Learn

Watch the video carefully. Pay attention to the characters, mysterious events, important clues, conversations, and how the mysteries are investigated.

💡

Detective Tip: Listen carefully to the conversations. Notice important details, suspicious situations, and evidence that may help explain each mystery.

Mission 2

Answer the Questions Orally

Complete all 10 speaking questions in English. Use information from the video to explain your ideas, analyze the mysteries, and give your own opinions. Speak clearly and provide supporting reasons or examples.

🎯

How to Complete This Mission: Click Start Speaking and allow microphone access. Answer each question in English. Check the displayed transcript before clicking Check My Answer.

Click the microphone and answer in English.

Click the microphone and answer in English.

⭐ Speaking Tips

  • Speak clearly and confidently.
  • Use complete English sentences.
  • Explain your ideas with reasons and examples.
  • Use appropriate vocabulary related to mysteries and investigations.
  • Pay attention to grammar and pronunciation.
  • Think critically before giving your conclusions.
  • Do not answer with only one or two words.
